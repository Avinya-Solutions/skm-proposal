04 / From Booking to Entry 04 / 10

Professional ticketing.
A smooth welcome at the door.

Flexible booking for members and guests, with clear entry validation for the event team.

From Online Booking to Validated Entry Multi-Desk Live Attendance Sync
✓ VALID • Entry recorded

Confirms ticket category, entitlements and remaining family admissions.

! ALREADY USED • Review

Flags prior check-in with timestamp and desk operator for quick review.

✕ INVALID / CANCELLED • No entry

Automatically blocks cancelled, refunded, invalid or fully used tickets.

Ticketing Check-in and entry control
Member, guest, student, volunteer and early-bird pricing. Authorised volunteers scan QR tickets using a phone.
Family bookings with a defined admission count. Record arrivals against the permitted count, including partial family arrivals.
Optional patron contributions and event add-ons. Show ticket category and any included entitlements.
Capacity limits, booking confirmations and ticket access. Validate the event, payment status and remaining admissions.
Cancellation and refund status linked to tickets. Block cancelled, refunded, invalid and fully used tickets.
Confirmed bookings automatically email QR tickets and event details; tickets also remain in the dashboard. Search by booking reference or name when a QR cannot be presented.
One live attendance record

Multiple entry desks share the same admission count. Each check-in records the operator and time. Authorised corrections require a reason; re-entry follows the event’s policy.
